# Farkel Turn Strategy — Executive Summary

This looks at **one turn of Farkel** and answers one question: \
**When should you bank vs. keep rolling?**

## Key result: a simple “banking threshold” rule

Use one threshold per dice count: if your **current turn points** are at/above the threshold for the dice you’re about to roll, **bank**. Otherwise, **roll**.

**Recommended thresholds (points to bank at):**

If you’re about to roll:

- **1 die**: always bank, **never roll 1 die**
- **2 dice**: bank at **250** points
- **3 dice**: bank at **350** points
- **4 dice**: bank at **950** points
- **5 dice**: bank at **2800** points
- **6 dice**: never bank, **“always roll”**

## What you should expect

With this strategy, a turn averages about **~540 points**—but Farkel is swingy. Expect plenty of **0-point busts** and occasional **huge turns**.

## Quick mental model

- **Few dice left (1–3)** → protect your points.
- **Many dice (5–6)** → take advantage and push unless you're risking *a lot* of points.
